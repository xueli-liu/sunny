Biography

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Cybersecurity at Tianjin University. I earned my Ph.D. from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2018 under the supervision of Professor Jianzhong Li. From 2015 to 2017, I was a joint Ph.D. student at the University of Edinburgh, mentored by Academician Wenfei Fan. Her research spans database systems and theory, big data processing, data governance, graph data mining, and data security and privacy computing. Currently, she focuses on exploring the integration of logical rules with deep learning and large-scale models, aiming to tackle knowledge discovery challenges in multimodal data environments and to enhance the interpretability of AI computation results. In addition, she conducts in-depth research on data security and privacy computing in AI for Data, as well as the design and development of biosynthetic enzymes in AI for Science.
