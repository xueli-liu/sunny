Biography

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Cybersecurity at Tianjin University. I earned my Ph.D. from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2018 under the supervision of Professor Jianzhong Li. From 2015 to 2017, I was a joint Ph.D. student at the University of Edinburgh, mentored by Academician Wenfei Fan. My research interests include database systems, big data processing, data governance, graph data mining, and multimodal data analysis. Currently, I focus on integrating logical rules with deep learning for knowledge discovery in multimodal data and enhancing AI interpretability. I also work on large-scale models for biomanufacturing.
